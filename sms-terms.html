The program

By entering your mobile number on thesaintwood.com and tapping “Text it to me”, you agree to receive recurring automated marketing text messages from Saintwood (5697 S Jones Blvd, Suite 160, Las Vegas, NV 89118), including your opening-week free coffee (“First Pour”), opening news, menu updates and offers. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Frequency & cost

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for details.

Opting out & help

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You’ll get one final message confirming you’ve been removed. Reply HELP for help, or email hello@thesaintwood.com.

The free coffee

One free coffee per mobile number, redeemable in store during opening week only by showing the text we send you. Not transferable, no cash value. Details of the drink and the dates will be in the text.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We don’t sell or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in consent with third parties for their marketing. See our Privacy Policy.
